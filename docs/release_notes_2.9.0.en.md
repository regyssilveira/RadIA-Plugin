# Release notes - RadIA 2.9.0

> **Status:** release candidate on August 12, 2026.

RadIA 2.9.0 makes the Delphi experience more contextual, safe, and verifiable. This version documents
148 internal tools.

## Delphi context and knowledge

- sanitized IDE, architecture, project, framework, configuration, package, and library profile;
- curated, versioned, filterable, and citable Delphi guidance;
- mentor that explains selected code for beginner, cross-language, and experienced profiles.

## Designer, auditing, and modernization

- bidirectional DFM/PAS audit for orphan components, declarations, and events;
- before/after Form Designer snapshots and visual comparison with an explicit decision;
- reversible BDE, ADO, and dbExpress migration to FireDAC with build and test gates;
- later DEXT and form-decomposition planning without risky automatic rewriting.

## Governance and evidence

- autonomous execution contract with limits, pause, resume, checkpoints, and rollback;
- corporate security brief comparing local and remote routes and the limits of their guarantees;
- deterministic local benchmark for success, time, cost, and rollback without telemetry.

## Compatibility

- Delphi 12 Win32, Delphi 13 Win32, and Delphi 13 IDE64 remain the supported targets;
- bilingual operational documentation and the 148-tool catalog are synchronized.

See the [release audit](release_audit_2.9.0.en.md) for final gates and evidence.
