# RadIA roadmap

The roadmap describes direction, not release history. Published deliveries are documented in
[GitHub Releases](https://github.com/regyssilveira/RadIA-Plugin/releases); executable work belongs in
the [backlog](backlog.en.md).

## Current direction

Maintain and deepen the complete Delphi 12 and 13 experience through real usage: create, understand,
build, test, and debug projects without requiring users to know the internal architecture. Future
evolution must first demonstrate an observable gain for this flow and have verifiable acceptance
criteria.

## Active cycle

The current cycle develops the **FireDAC Advisor** as a complete Delphi data-engineering capability.
It starts with contracts and tests, advances through inventory and deterministic analyzers, integrates
AI explanations and generation, and finishes with reversible mutations, migration, and Delphi 12 and
13 E2E validation. Executable scope and gates remain in the [backlog](backlog.en.md).

Delphi 11, C++, Lazarus, marketplace work, a public extension repository, WebView replacement, and
DCU reading remain outside the current direction.
