# Cache management

Open **Tools > RadIA > Rad IA Cache Manager** to inspect the local AI response cache.

The panel shows the entry identifier, character count, creation time, and last use. Response content is not
displayed, preventing accidental disclosure. **Remove selected** deletes only the selected entry; **Clear all**
deletes every entry. Both operations require confirmation, and future requests rebuild what they need.

Closing the panel or using **Refresh** does not change the cache. Cleanup does not alter projects, chats, or
credentials.
